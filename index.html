Before you begin

A few good questions.

Why founding-client pricing?

This is a new training offering. These introductory prices acknowledge that we are establishing the program’s delivery track record. We ask for candid feedback as we refine it. Testimonials and case studies are optional and require separate approval.

Which program should we choose?

Choose Foundations for team confidence and guided practice. Choose Build when you want agreed workflows built and tested with the team. Choose Executive 1:1 for a private setup focused on one person’s work.

What do we need before sessions begin?

A suitable Hermes installation, model-provider access, and approval to use the relevant tools and sample data. We assess readiness during intake. Installation support beyond the agreed scope is quoted separately.

What is not included?

Software subscriptions, model/API usage, hardware, travel, custom connector development, major data cleanup, and ongoing maintenance are excluded unless explicitly included in the written scope. This is not a security or compliance certification.

Will the assistant act without our approval?

Permission boundaries are agreed before connecting tools. We favor read-only access and drafts where appropriate. Sending messages, publishing content, or changing records requires explicit authorization. Scheduled workflows need a suitable runtime; schedules do not remove the need for oversight.

Is post-program support unlimited?

No. The scope of work specifies the support channel, total allowance, response expectations, and end date. Additional work is separately agreed, so everyone knows what is included.

Are you the official Hermes Agent training provider?

No. This is an independent training offering for users of Hermes Agent. It is not presented as affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Nous Research.
